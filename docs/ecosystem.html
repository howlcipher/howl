One Ecosystem. Clear Roles. エコシステム // 2026

Explore possibilities. Keep evidence. Control consequential actions. Howl brings specialized tools together around a common rule: intent is not authority. Each component owns a distinct problem so creative work, factual claims, orchestration and execution do not silently become the same thing.

AXIOM // INTENT IS NOT AUTHORITY. Probabilistic intelligence proposes changes; deterministic machinery strictly verifies constraints; human operators retain sovereign authority over consequential risk.

SECTION // 01 Proposals Do Not Authorize Effects

ECO-MOD-ARCHITECTURE

Architecture / conceptual relationships. The arrows below describe responsibilities, not a claim that every pair has a native runtime adapter.

Human objective → HowlPlane

Planning, agent routing, evidence and policy evaluation.

HowlCreate ↔ HowlDream

Intentional invention and controlled speculative experiments. Candidates return for evaluation.

HowlFrame → HowlChangeOps

Capability-bounded runtime and deterministic policy underpin governed change execution.

HowlRelay carries work state and handoffs. HowlWriter communicates reviewed knowledge. HowlDream currently exports advisory artifacts only.

Dream output is data, not authority. HowlDream cannot deploy, merge, approve or send communications. Its WAKE checks are scoped to supplied facts, source IDs and arithmetic. HowlPlane evaluates work; applicable human and HowlChangeOps gates still govern consequential actions.

Two kinds of exploration

HowlCreate invents intentionally: it searches for useful concepts through deliberate creative operators and convergence.

HowlDream explores speculatively: it treats outputs as experimental material, compares conditions with a baseline, induces failures, and preserves unresolved claims after checks.

HowlDream intentionally explores possibilities that may be incorrect, measures known model failures, and verifies speculative ideas within explicit limits before downstream evaluation. A candidate can deserve investigation while remaining unverified.
